## Reflection

### 1. What went well during this unit?

Our group worked together effectively to establish the core app description and app purpose. We quickly aligned on the conceptual definition of our target game mechanics and agreed on the explicit terminology for our word relationship tags.

### 2. What were some challenges your group faced in this unit?

The most challenging part of developing our app concept was realizing that if we showed answer choices in Chinese characters, a Chinese speaker would instantly know the answer. We fixed this challenge by deciding to show the multiple-choice options as English meanings so that the quiz game remains hard.

### 3. What additional support will you need in upcoming units as you continue to work on your final project?

We will need additional support in designing our database schema layout for Milestone 2. Specifically, we will need assistance formatting our tables to clean up relational linkages for our custom vocabulary decks and tracking player history data correctly.
